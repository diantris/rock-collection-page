Rock Collection

Hello, this is my personal collection of rocks, minerals and other shinies. Many of them are unidentified, or only tentatively identified: each page shows the photos and what has been observed and measured so far.

They are often not what the seller claimed it to be, but in many cases I knew and bought them anyway, because I like them. So don't worry about me being "scammed" - but I would still like to know, what it is, that I actually have. :D

If you know what one of them is, or have an idea, please mention its number (#…) when you talk to me.
